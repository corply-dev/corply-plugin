# Corply

Form a Delaware C-Corp, resume a saved application, import an existing company, and manage company documents, ownership records, governance and compliance through conversation. Corply is software, not a law firm, and does not provide legal, tax or accounting advice.

## What this plugin connects to

This plugin connects only to Corply’s hosted MCP server at https://corply.dev/mcp/claude. It contains one skill and no local executable code, hooks, installers or credential-reading scripts. The declared connector receives the company details, founder information, documents and requested actions you authorize Claude to send. OAuth authentication and token refresh are handled by the host. Never paste passwords, access tokens, government identifiers or payment credentials into chat.

## Getting started

Add the plugin, connect Corply, and sign in through Corply’s OAuth page. Then ask “Set up a DE C Corp for us,” “Continue my company application,” or “Show my cap table.” The agent verifies the connected account and asks for missing information. Signatures, invitations, purchases and filing handoffs require the relevant person’s approval. Any Corply service fee is paid by the founder on Corply’s checkout page; the plugin does not transfer money or open real bank accounts. This skill focuses on company formation and records and does not initiate bank onboarding.

## Privacy and support

Company data is held by Corply according to its privacy policy, including retention required for corporate records and legal obligations. This plugin stores no company data locally.
